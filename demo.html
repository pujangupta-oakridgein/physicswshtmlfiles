MathType opens from the Σ toolbar control. The two Desmos calculators are separate tools. Graphing Calculator inserts a genuine PNG using Desmos' official screenshot API. Scientific Calculator attempts a native capture if available and otherwise captures the rendered DOM; if cross-origin browser restrictions block that capture, it reports the limitation and inserts nothing. Both calculator features must be enabled for your key. For a Vite host, configure VITE_DESMOS_API_KEY or supply window.__MYP_CONFIG__.DESMOS_API_KEY at runtime.
